Terms

These pages describe a personal, unofficial study organizer. They do not make the project an official service of SchoolEverywhere, Google, or any school.

Personal use and control

The importer is intended for its operator’s personal organization of authorized school references. The operator chooses what accounts and sources to connect, reviews local classifications, and controls whether eligible links are organized in Classroom.

The operator is responsible for having the necessary access rights and for following the rules that apply to their Google account, school sources, original files, and Classroom classes. Access rights, provider rules, and source availability may change.

Practical limits

Source recognition, links, classifications, provider availability, and Google operations can be incomplete or change. Check source material and destination content yourself. No particular result, uninterrupted availability, or successful Google OAuth review or publishing status is promised.

The importer is not a tool for grading, deleting classes, completing quizzes, or submitting homework. Original files remain at their source; the importer does not upload file copies.
