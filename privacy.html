Privacy

The importer is designed to run locally on its operator’s Windows computer. This static information site has no analytics, forms, or app login.

Information the local importer handles

With the operator’s authorization, the importer reads Google Drive references and Classroom course, materials, coursework, topic, and roster metadata. Creating a course uses an additional Google course-management permission. Google bundles some permission descriptions more broadly than the importer’s features: the app does not grade work, delete classes, complete quizzes, or submit homework.

Item metadata, original links, local classifications and review decisions, publication records, and provider usage history are stored on the local computer. Windows DPAPI protects locally stored keys and Google credentials. This is a description of the design, not a guarantee of perfect security.

AI providers

For classification, selected bounded samples of lesson text or media may be sent to Gemini. Compatible text may be sent to Groq as a backup. Each provider processes requests under its own terms and privacy rules. Review Google’s privacy policy and Groq’s privacy policy. Do not rely on this page for a promise about provider retention or training.

Credentials and Zoom join secrets are not intended as classifier inputs. The information-site host does not receive school records through the site itself. The hosting platform may process ordinary access metadata; see GitHub’s general privacy statement.

Your controls

The operator can pause local checks, disconnect the importer, and revoke Google access at Google Account permissions. Local records can be removed from the operator’s computer. Provider-side handling remains subject to each provider’s own terms and controls.
