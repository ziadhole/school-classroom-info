SchoolEveryWhere Automation

An unofficial personal study organizer, independent of SchoolEverywhere and Google.

What it is for

The planned local importer reads authorized Grade10-B school and Google Drive references, recognizes individual files, and organizes their original links into separate Google Classroom subject classes. It can create materials and assignments from source details.

It links to original files. It does not upload copies of files to Drive or Classroom. This information site is separate from the private local importer; hosting this site does not receive the importer’s school records.

The importer’s complete operation and any Google OAuth publishing or verification outcome have not been established by this site.
